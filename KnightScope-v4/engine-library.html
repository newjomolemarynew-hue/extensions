Additional engines

PlentyChess, Berserk, Reckless, Koivisto, Ethereal, Caissa, Lc0, Viridithas and Stormphrax are not bundled as working integrations yet. They will only become selectable after their browser-compatible engine files, licences and adapters are added and tested.

Performance

V4 retains the existing worker-based Stockfish setup. The laptop-friendly target is one active engine at a time, with analysis workers shut down when no longer needed.
